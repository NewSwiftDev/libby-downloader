Turn on Libby Downloader in Settings, then Safari, then Extensions. Chapters are stored in the app group group.com.libbydownloader.safari. Use the button below to copy them into Files. If that group is unavailable, Safari saves flat audio files in Downloads.

Turn on Libby Downloader in Safari Settings. Chapters are written to ~/Downloads/libby-downloads/<title>/chapter-NNN.mp3 with metadata.json.

Libby Downloader is on. Open a borrowed audiobook, let it play for a few seconds, then use the download button in the player. Files land in ~/Downloads/libby-downloads.

Libby Downloader is off. Turn it on in Safari Settings, then open a borrowed audiobook.
